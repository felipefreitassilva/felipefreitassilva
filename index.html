Uma Porto Alegre Alemã

A multilingual digital map of Porto Alegre's German architectural heritage, built for PUCRS Architecture faculty as part of a Brazil-Germany cultural cooperation effort.

Why it matters Supports institutional cooperation between Rio Grande do Sul and the German states of Hessen and Rheinland-Pfalz. The map covers the works of the architect Theodor Wiederspahn, each with its own page for history, stylistic analysis, drawings and photographs, plus a CMS so the clients can maintain it themselves. Designed mobile first, because the expected use is on site, standing in front of the buildings. I was the technical coordinator - the final stage of PUCRS's four-semester AGES track - running backlog, squads and stakeholder communication while still reviewing code and building the Google Maps route integration.

Client PUCRS Architecture faculty, supporting Rio Grande do Sul - Germany institutional cooperation (March - June 2026)
